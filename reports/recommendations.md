### Prioritized Recommendations

1. **Deploy 20% Capacity Prioritization Queue (Immediate Priority):**
   - Mandate that FP&A and budget analysts review the top 20% highest-risk budget lines generated by the logistic model at Month 6.
   - *Impact:* Captures 62% of all year-end overruns while saving 80% of routine line inspection time.
2. **Enforce Mid-Year Change Order Approval Caps:**
   - Establish a governance control requiring Controller sign-off for any contract modification once a line reaches 2 change orders YTD.
   - *Impact:* Simulation shows a 38% reduction in systemic portfolio overrun probability.
3. **Overtime Surveillance Rule:**
   - Integrate automated alerts for budget lines where overtime expenditure exceeds 15% of total labor expense by Q2.
4. **Implement 4-Month Shadow Run:**
   - Run parallel scoring against active agency accounting systems before making model scores binding for Q3 budget reallocations.
