## 2. Methodology & Key Value Drivers

1. **Early Overrun Remediation:** Catching budget overruns at mid-year allows department heads to freeze non-essential spend, renegotiate vendor contracts, and curtail overtime before deficits become unrecoverable.
2. **Targeted Analyst Labor:** Reviewing only the top 20% of budget lines captures >62% of overruns, freeing hundreds of analyst hours for strategic FP&A forecasting.
3. **Conservative Payback:** Under Base Case assumptions, the initial implementation cost is recovered within **1.4 months**, delivering a **Year 1 Net Benefit of over $3.0M** across a $1.2B budget pool.
