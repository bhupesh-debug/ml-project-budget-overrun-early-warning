# Data Quality and Limitations Report
## Budget Overrun Early-Warning Model
**Author:** Bhupesh Chandra Dimri | Email: bhupesh7cd@gmail.com | October 2026

---

### Part 1: Data Quality Validation
- **Total Records:** 3,200 checkpoints across 4 fiscal years (FY1 to FY4).
- **Missing Values:** 0 null values across all 12 operational features.
- **Outlier Boundaries:** Spend percentages clipped to [5%, 110%]; ratios verified in [0, 1].
- **Class Balance:** 25.0% positive overrun rate (800 / 3,200), representative of historical agency stress.
- **Temporal Order:** Strict time-based holdout evaluation: trained on FY1-3 (2,361 rows), tested on FY4 (839 rows). Zero look-ahead contamination.

---

### Part 2: Limitations & Known Risks
1. **Synthetic Generator Structure:** Data is synthetic and generated by a parametric process. While it validates the end-to-end econometric and simulation pipeline, model parameters must be recalibrated when connected to live ERP systems.
2. **Exogenous Economic Shocks:** Unbudgeted collective bargaining wage increases or macroeconomic supply inflation occurring in H2 cannot be predicted solely from mid-year checkpoint features.
3. **Observational vs. Causal Interpretation:** Regression odds ratios indicate statistical associations, not guaranteed causal outcomes. Capping change orders should be accompanied by operational workflow reviews.
