Q-P1-001 Which factors are most strongly associated with overrun risk?
▾
Direct Answer: Prior-year budget variance (Odds Ratio 4.36x), year-to-date change orders (Odds Ratio 2.95x), overtime share (Odds Ratio 2.45x), and non-labor spending share are the primary statistical determinants.
Evidence: Logistic regression parameter estimates ($N=2,361, p < 0.001$).
Recommendation: Institute mandatory change-order reviews whenever a contract line reaches 3 modifications.
Q-P1-002 Which model performs best out of sample?
▾
Direct Answer: Logistic Regression performs best, achieving ROC-AUC 0.906 and PR-AUC 0.777 on the FY2025 holdout, slightly edging out HistGradientBoosting (ROC-AUC 0.899) and outperforming the rule baseline (0.686).
Evidence: Time-based holdout evaluation across 839 budget lines.
Q-P1-003 Does Logistic Regression outperform the existing heuristic?
▾
Direct Answer: Yes, decisively. It provides a +32.1% improvement in ROC-AUC and captures 62.1% of overruns in the top 20% review volume compared to only 35.5% for the spend-burn gap rule.
Q-P1-004 What alert threshold provides a reasonable operational trade-off?
▾
Direct Answer: An alert threshold of $p \ge 0.42$ (top 20% review volume) captures 62.1% of overruns with 77.7% precision, minimizing analyst review fatigue while preventing cost leakage.
