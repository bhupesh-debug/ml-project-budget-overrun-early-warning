# Governance, Internal Controls & Auditability Framework
## Budget Overrun Early-Warning Model (SOX 404 & SR 11-7)
**Author:** Bhupesh Chandra Dimri | Email: bhupesh7cd@gmail.com | October 2026

---

### 1. Internal Control Objectives
1. **Mid-Year Checkpoint Governance:** Budget lines exceeding the 20% risk cutoff require mandatory written mitigation plans from department heads within 10 business days.
2. **Change Order Controls:** Any contract modification resulting in > 2 change orders YTD requires secondary approval from the Agency Controller.
3. **Overtime Thresholds:** Overtime exceeding 20% of total labor expenditure triggers automated FP&A audit notifications.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Override Policy:** Automated risk scores guide review priority; budgetary freezes or de-obligations require executive committee authorization.

### 2. Ongoing Model Monitoring
- Monthly Population Stability Index (PSI) tracking feature drift.
- Semi-annual model recalibration incorporating finalized fiscal year-end actuals.
